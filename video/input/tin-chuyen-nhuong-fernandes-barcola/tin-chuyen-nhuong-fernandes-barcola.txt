Tin chuyen nhuong: Man Utd dan dau cuoc dua gianh Mateus Fernandes

Manchester United dang vuot len truoc Arsenal, Paris Saint-Germain va Real Madrid trong cuoc dua gianh tien ve Mateus Fernandes cua West Ham, giua mua he chuyen nhuong soi dong voi hang loat thuong vu o tuyen giua va khung thanh.

## Key facts
- Manchester United dan truoc Arsenal, PSG va Real Madrid trong vu Mateus Fernandes.
- Fernandes 21 tuoi, tien ve cua West Ham, duoc danh gia cao o chau Au du bi gat khoi danh sach World Cup cua Bo Dao Nha.
- West Ham can ban cau thu thu ve hon 100 trieu bang truoc cuoi thang de tuan thu luat cong bang tai chinh, sau khi xuong hang.
- United chua chao gia chinh thuc nhung da dam phan voi phia cau thu; dieu khoan ca nhan duoc cho la khong thanh van de.
- United co the chieu mo ba tien ve he nay, chi hon 100 trieu bang qua nhieu thuong vu.
- Cac muc tieu gom Carlos Baleba cua Brighton, Alex Scott cua Bournemouth, Fernandes, va Ederson da ky tu Atalanta.
- United thua cuoc dua gianh Elliot Anderson cua Nottingham Forest vao tay Manchester City, nen nham toi cac phuong an de tiep can hon.
- Arsenal va Liverpool theo doi Bradley Barcola cua PSG sau khi co tin canh ve nguoi Phap co the ra di.
- Barcola tut lai sau Khvicha Kvaratskhelia trong thu bac o PSG, dam phan gia han hop dong be tac.
- Du HLV Luis Enrique muon giu Barcola, PSG van san sang lang nghe loi de nghi.
- Newcastle United can nhac thu mon James Trafford cua Manchester City, nguoi it duoc ra san sau khi Gianluigi Donnarumma cap ben.
- Trafford la thu mon nguoi Anh; Newcastle danh gia phuong an khung thanh ngoai thu mon so mot hien tai Nick Pope.

## Context
- Ederson tu Atalanta la tan binh tuyen giua dau tien United chot trong he nay.
- Viec mat Anderson vao tay Man City buoc United lap danh sach rut gon Baleba, Scott va Fernandes lam phuong an thay the.
- Suc ep tai chinh sau khi xuong hang khien West Ham buoc phai ban tru cot, mo co hoi cho ben mua.
- O PSG, su vuon len cua Kvaratskhelia day Barcola xuong nhom phia sau, lam tuong lai anh bo ngo.
