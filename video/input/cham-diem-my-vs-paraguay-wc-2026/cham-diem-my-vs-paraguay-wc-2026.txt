Cham diem My vs Paraguay: Balogun lap cu dup, My pha ky luc World Cup

Folarin Balogun lap cu dup trong ngay tuyen My nghien nat Paraguay 4-1 o tran ra quan World Cup 2026, lap ky luc ghi ban nhieu nhat lich su mot tran World Cup cua My.

## Dien bien chinh
- My thang Paraguay 4-1 trong tran khai man, choi ap dao gan nhu tron tran.
- Phut 7, Pulisic va McKennie phoi hop tao pha phan luoi nha, mo ty so cho My.
- Phut 31, Pulisic kien tao de Balogun dut diem mot cham nang ty so len 2-0.
- Cuoi hiep mot, Balogun nhan duong chuyen cua Malik Tillman, di qua hai hau ve roi sut tung goc cao, hoan tat cu dup.
- Phut 73, My de thua mot ban kho chap nhan, Mauricio ghi ban rut ngan cach biet cho Paraguay.
- Phut bu gio cuoi tran, Gio Reyna an dinh chien thang 4-1 bang mot sieu pham.
- 4 ban la so ban nhieu nhat My tung ghi trong mot tran World Cup, pha ky luc ton tai tu nam 1930.

- Folarin Balogun — 9/10: Mau tien dao cam dang cap. Dut diem, di bong, chon thoi diem deu hoan hao, mot minh ganh hang cong va lap cu dup.
