Cham diem Canada vs Bosnia: Larin giai cuu ngay ra quan World Cup 2026

Vao san tu bang ghe du bi, Cyle Larin ghi ban go hoa 1-1 truoc Bosnia and Herzegovina, mang ve cho Canada diem so dau tien trong lich su du World Cup ngay ra quan tren san Toronto.

## Dien bien chinh
- Canada choi lan luot phan lon thoi gian nhung bi dan truoc giua hiep 1.
- Lovo Jukic danh dau can thanh ghi ban cho Bosnia, sau pha danh dau noi cua Sead Kolasinac.
- Truoc khi thua ban, Jonathan David bo lo co hoi ngon an, sut thang vao thu mon Nikola Vasilj.
- Phut 75, Larin vao san thay nguoi va ghi ban gan nhu ngay lap tuc, dut diem trong vong cam co chut may man khi bong cham chan hau ve doi huong.
- Larin nhan duong chuyen mot cham cua dong doi vao san Promise David, khong che bong roi tung luoi.
- Cuoi tran Larin suyt an dinh chien thang nhung bi hang thu Bosnia can pha xuat sac.
- Thu mon Maxime Crepeau co pha cuu thua quan trong khi Ermedin Demirovic thoat xuong doi mat khung thanh.
- Richie Laryea suyt ghi ban, cu sut bi Kolasinac pha bat len xa ngang.
- Tani Oluwaseyi danh dau cham dich nhung bi Nikola Katic pha tren vach voi.

## Context
- Day la diem so dau tien trong lich su du World Cup cua Canada, sau chuoi tran thua o cac ky truoc.
- Larin la tien dao mua truoc choi cho Southampton theo dang cho muon.
- Jonathan David, chan sut so mot moi thoi dai cua Canada, dang khoac ao Juventus va bi danh gia la lam an chua tuong xung.
- Truoc tran gap Qatar, Marsch dung truoc bai toan lon: Larin co le xung dang da chinh thay Jonathan David.
